**ADR-0041 amends ADR-0040's D2 to make period-label visibility measured-fit.** ADR-0040 let the user choose `MM` or `MMM`, but both forms were gated by the same fixed `MinimumHeaderLabelWidthPt` floor, so `MM` showed on identical intervals to `MMM` — cosmetic, not functional. ADR-0041 replaces that floor **for the period band** with a measured-fit test (show iff the interval is at least as wide as the label measures), so `MM` — measuring shorter — survives on narrower intervals. The band stays uniform: the format is still one verbatim, user-chosen form, only the show/hide decision varies, and this is measured-fit, not ADR-0039's rejected per-interval ladder. The year band keeps the floor; measurement failure falls back to it.

**ADR-0040 supersedes ADR-0039's automatic MMM→MM fit ladder.** The owner rejected the per-interval step-down on review — it makes the band non-uniform (some months at `MMM`, some at `MM`) and is effectively unobservable in live use, because a three-character month at the 8pt measuring seam is narrower than the 18pt `MinimumHeaderLabelWidthPt` suppression floor. The selected `PeriodLabelFormat` is instead surfaced directly as a Plot Time Scale dropdown (Month scale: `MM` or `MMM`) and emitted verbatim, so the format is a uniform, explicit choice. ADR-0039 is retained as history.

**R5.16a ribbon layout and icon ruling (owner, 2026-11-12).** Recorded here rather than as an ADR because it is reversible presentation with no data or architecture consequence. The Gantt Creator tab is re-organised by subject: **Ordering is merged into Outline** (Promote/Demote, Up/Down, Expand all/Collapse all in a two-wide, three-storey grid, because Promote/Demote are Excel's own outline group/ungroup operations); **Plot Area and Plot Time Scale are merged into Chart**; **Plot Size becomes Size** with one dropdown that shows and applies the stored preset, replacing four buttons; the width/height read-only edit boxes become `labelControl` readouts; **Style and Theme becomes Style**; the Labels captions shorten to Description / Date / Style; and **`grpPlaceholder`, labelled "Gantt Creator", becomes `grpSheet`, labelled Sheet**. The **Data** group keeps its owner-tooled structure: only the ruled labels **Repair** (was Fix) and **Elements** (was Additional elements) change, with control ids, menus, keytips and split buttons untouched. Every control now carries a **distinct** `imageMso`; a duplicate glyph is a test failure, and `scripts/check-ribbon-icons.ps1` verifies every id against the Office 2010 Icons Gallery and the `[MS-CUSTOMUI]` idMso table before the ribbon is accepted. The live-host render check, including `CalendarInsert`, remains owed and is recorded in the R5.16a work item.

**R5.19 ribbon icon mapping and palette (2026-11-16).** Recorded here rather than in the ADR because it is reversible presentation, while the *probe* it depends on is architectural and lives in ADR-0063. Every actionable control on the tab moves from `imageMso` to Lucide geometry served by one `getImage` callback, at 32 px on large controls and 16 px on normal controls and menu rows. The mapping is chosen so no two glyphs share a silhouette family at 16 px: the first draft put four "vertical line with arrows" glyphs on one tab and they were indistinguishable. Expand all and Collapse all therefore use `chevrons-up-down` / `chevrons-down-up` rather than `unfold-vertical` / `fold-vertical`, and Diagnostics uses `gauge` rather than a medical metaphor. Menu rows carry one family glyph per menu rather than a glyph per entity type, with the label carrying the variant. The four Size preset rows lose their orientation glyphs because `CT_Item` has no `getImage`, and the owner ruled the Size control itself stays icon-free: a text dropdown beside an inline label is crowded by a picture. The three add primaries serve a glyph that follows the same remembered menu pick their label follows, so a button reading Spacer cannot draw a delineator. Approving or reversing any of those mappings changes only `scripts/gen-ribbon-glyphs.ps1` and the generated catalogue — no schema, no scene, no adapter.

**R5.1a plot-date calendar pickers and never-stale plot-date text (owner, 2026-11-17).** Recorded here rather than as an ADR because it is reversible presentation plus two bounded behaviour fixes, with no schema, scene, renderer or Core change. **D1 — R5.20 wins:** a picked or typed plot date updates the box text and the button states immediately, and the chart rebuilds on the coalesced automatic pass only while `AutoRefreshEnabled` is on; with the switch off the chart waits for the explicit Refresh and the text stays consistent with what the chart shows. **D2 — native `MonthCalendar`** in a small tool-window popup anchored at the cursor, themed with the Office palette and **bypassed under Windows high contrast**, which also keeps month/year scrolling, keyboard navigation and screen-reader support free. **D3 — two buttons, one glyph:** both plot-date calendar buttons serve the same `calendar` asset, so the asset pair and the decode are shared; it is the single allowed duplicate in the ribbon-glyph uniqueness fact, and the label column plus the supertip carry the distinction. **D4 — `R5.1a`**, a slice of the landed R5.1 row rather than a new roadmap row. **D5 — a cleared date box reverts silently**, never switching the end to Auto: a revert costs one `InvalidateControl`, while Auto would cost a settings-table round-trip plus a full validate-and-rebuild pass and would duplicate the checkbox beside it. **D6 — the start-after-finish guard runs at input, before any write or pass, against the opposite end's EFFECTIVE date** — stored in explicit mode, data-derived in AUTO mode — so a start beyond the derived finish is refused in one line instead of failing the whole refresh with `StartAfterFinish`; the check previously skipped itself whenever the opposite end was automatic. The persistence path was revised with it: a plot commit writes only the four plot keys and a layout commit only the five layout keys, because the writer merges supplied keys over the table's current values and the old ten-key payload could overwrite a newer workbook margin, scale or preset with stale session state; and a refused or throwing write now restores the row it was about to commit and presents one actionable message, where it previously left the box showing a date the workbook did not hold and raised an operation-id error dialog.

**R5.16a add-entity menu clarity (owner, 2026-11-17).** Recorded here rather than as an ADR because it is reversible presentation: no schema, scene, adapter or Core change. Four rulings. **R1 — every section carries a titled divider, including the first.** The split-button primary renames itself to the last menu pick (R5.18g), so the button cannot describe the menu's first block; the owner's report was exactly that, and a `menuSeparator` with a `title` is the only heading a `menu` offers. **R2 — keytips read as basis + kind**, three characters at most: `AP`/`AB`/`BL`/`CU`/`CR` plus `A` activity, `P` procurement, `M` milestone, `I` interval, giving `APA`, `ABA`, `BLA`, `CUA`, `CRI`, `APP`, `ABP`, `BLP`, `APM`, `ABM`, `BLM`, `CRM`, `CUM`; the three element rows take their display name's abbreviation (`DEL`, `SPA`, `SPL`) and `DLY` carries Delay Event. Five draft codes were corrected because they broke that rule or took an invented prefix (`CIM`, `DLE`, `TD`, `TS`, `TP`), and the primaries keep `AA`, `AM`, `AD`. **R3 — tooltips state the differentiator, never restate the label.** The screentip names the concept ("Planned span") and the supertip carries at most two facts traced to an entity-guide section, under about 120 characters, because the host wraps a longer tip onto a third line. **R4 — three concept glyphs are funded: `procurement`, `span-delay`, `pen`.** At 16 px only a silhouette change survives, so the doubled-stroke procurement bar, the one-bar stepped delay and Lucide's diagonal pen were chosen; `palette` was rejected because its blob is already the Theme settings glyph, `swatch-book` because it reads as the Columns rectangles, and per-row variants for planned versus built versus baseline because those three are one bar with a different fill and nine near-identical silhouettes would be worse than nine identical ones. `critical` waits for the live pass. The approved wording, the rejected candidates and the raster argument are in [`plans/ribbon-add-entity-menu-clarity-plan.md`](../plans/ribbon-add-entity-menu-clarity-plan.md).

**R5.16a style-defined row glyph correction (owner live review, 2026-11-17).** The `pen` glyph proposed and first shipped for `Custom Activity` and `Custom Milestone` was rejected on the rendered surface: at 16 px its nib reads as a padlock beside the two genuine padlock glyphs, and on this tab a padlock states that the cells or the chart are locked, so the misreading is a state contradiction rather than a near-miss. `circle-dashed` — Lucide's draft ring — is adopted for both rows instead, which also states the concept more honestly: the row's style is not yet chosen. The three concept primaries follow the swap through `AddEntityPrimaryGlyph`, the checker's primary-glyph set names the ring, and the pen assets are deleted rather than left orphaned. This supersedes R4's `pen` choice in the entry above; the rest of R4 stands.

## ADR template

```markdown
# ADR-<NNNN> — <title>

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-<NNNN>
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Context**: the forces at play, the constraint, the question.
- **Decision**: what we decided.
- **Consequences**: trade-offs, what becomes easier, what becomes harder.
- **Alternatives considered**: what we rejected, and why.
```
