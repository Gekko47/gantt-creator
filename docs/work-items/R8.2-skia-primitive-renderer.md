  `AutoOpen` render in the registered packed XLL threw
  `TypeInitializationException → SkiaApi` (SkiaSharp's default loader
  does not resolve the packed `NATIVE_LIBRARY_LZMA`). With
  `libSkiaSharp.dll` placed **beside the packed XLL**, the same in-host
  render passed: marker `OK:4.157.0.0:c:\…\publish\libSkiaSharp.dll`.
- **Chosen deployment route (D5):** ship `libSkiaSharp.dll` beside the
  XLL — R10.2 copies it, R10.7 records the MIT notice. The stop
  condition ("native assets cannot ship in the add-in payload") is
  **not** triggered: they ship, by the beside-the-XLL route rather than
  packed-native extraction.
- **Alternatives assessed (best-option check):** `System.Drawing.Common`
  11.0.0 (MIT, GDI+) is weaker on determinism — AA/ClearType and DPI
  behaviour depend on host settings, and the golden-image policy needs
  pinned AA; `SixLabors.ImageSharp` 4.1.3 + Drawing 3.1.3 is pure

  commercial-use risk) and Drawing lags the core version; Direct2D/WIC
  needs raw COM interop; WPF `RenderTargetBitmap` needs a dispatcher in
  the add-in. **SkiaSharp remains the best fit** (MIT, .NET 10 asset,
  CPU raster with pinned AA, integrated PNG encode, HarfBuzz for the
  R8.3 text route).
- **Dependency-policy obligation:** the dependency materially shapes

## Design decisions (behavioural)

- **D1 — Consume, never recalculate.** The renderer walks the scene's
  ordered primitives; geometry converts through the single raster
  mapping (architecture formula); it never re-derives dates, lanes,
  colours, or order (contract-tested the R4.3 D4 way).
- **D2 — Z-order is the scene's.** Primitives draw in scene order
  back-to-front; no renderer-side sorting beyond that order; a
  contract test pins the draw sequence.
- **D3 — Clipping per the scene.** Clipped primitives draw their
  clip (the scene already clipped); the renderer applies the canvas
  clip at the scene bounds — no renderer-side policy.
- **D4 — Deterministic re-render.** Two renders of the same scene +
  width produce byte-identical pixels (the determinism pin; golden
  comparisons build on it). The determinism metadata names the
  **CPU raster surface** (no GPU) and the pinned antialias settings —
  the two inputs that could otherwise vary across machines.
- **D5 — Native assets.** The chosen SkiaSharp version's native
  binaries are part of the add-in payload — the packaging row (R10.2)
  inherits this requirement and R10.7's SBOM/third-party-notices
  freeze records the licence; the earlier "R9.5" label here was a
  stale row reference (R9.5 is the accessibility row), corrected
  2026-11-22.
