# Work item — R5.12

> **Guide status**: Authored (Tier B — binding contract) 2026-10-09 ·
> Depends on: R3.5 (frame/band primitives), ADR-0035 (label width is
> bounded by available space).

## Outcome

When the Month scale is selected with the MMM format and a period's
three-character label measures wider than its visible interval, the
period header emits the two-digit MM form instead — unconditionally,
so an MM that still does not fit is clipped by the existing engine
behaviour. Every other scale/format pair, the year band, and the
suppression rule are unchanged.

## Context and owed deferrals

Roadmap row: *Add the month period-label fit ladder (MMM → MM when
the band is too narrow) — Ladder tests for the step-down, the
wide-band control, the MM-also-clips ruling, the scale/format scope,
and suppression precedence; label IDs unchanged — Required: narrow a
month plot until labels step down and confirm `09`-style labels
render.*

The entity guide §6 previously defined only the suppression rule
("suppress rather than overlap when too narrow" below
`MinimumHeaderLabelWidthPt`). The owner ruling of 2026-10-09 adds the
step-down for Month + MMM only, and settles the end condition: the
step is unconditional, and a still-too-narrow MM clips with the
existing engine rather than suppressing or keeping MMM.
