## Design decisions (behavioural)

- **D1 — Pure fit, applied once.** The fit math is Core-pure and
  unit-tested; the adapter applies the single computed
  position/scale; a second scaling call anywhere fails the sequence
  pin (**positive test**).
- **D2 — Ratio preserved by construction.** Scale = min(fitWidth/
  groupWidth, fitHeight/groupHeight); the applied size is
  scale·groupSize (both axes) — the ratio cannot drift; the pin
  asserts the applied aspect equals the export aspect within
  tolerance for every slide size.
- **D3 — The matrix is 4:3, 16:9, custom.** Custom = arbitrary
  slide dimensions (incl. extreme aspect slides); margins from the
  recorded margin policy; every case pins position (within margins,
  deterministic placement) + scale + ratio.

## Stop conditions (item-specific)

- A slide size distorts the group (host auto-fit interference) →
  stop with evidence; the once-rule may need an ADR amendment, not a
  quiet second scaling.
- The margin policy needs a product number → stop; record the
  proposal; the human picks.

## Risk and rollback

single application — the live per-size gate catches it. Rollback:
`git revert`.
