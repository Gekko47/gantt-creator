## Design decisions (spike protocol)

- **D1 — Measure, then propose.** Per Office build, record: the exact
  clipboard format set present after copy; whether each format
  survives staging deletion (immediately and after a bounded delay);
  whether Excel/PowerPoint paste still materialises the group with
  expected count/bounds; whether ungroup/edit works. The ADR proposes
  the simple delete path (architecture step 7) or the
  staging-workbook fallback **from this evidence only**.
- **D2 — Deterministic assertions, not vibes.** Every claim is a
  recorded assertion (format values, shape counts, bounds within the
  documented tolerance) — reproducible per build.
- **D3 — Human approval gate.** The ADR outcome is approved before
  R6.5 (staging lifecycle) and R6.6 (clipboard) are implemented —
  their designs depend on it.
- **D4 — No orphan Office processes** (the `verify-office.ps1`
  ownership-sweep discipline; the spike runs under the same harness).
