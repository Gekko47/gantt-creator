## Defect fix — collapsed rows (2026-11-19)

The one-click gate above ran on a fixture with no collapsed section. The owner's
workbook has one, and **Copy editable refused on every attempt** while Refresh chart
rendered it normally. Diagnosed from the add-in log, not from the dialog:

- Four attempts (08:00:43, 08:00:50, 08:01:28, 08:02:51) each logged
  `gantt.protection window open released=True`, then `gantt.shape.create-steps n=24`,
  then the window close with no success record — so protection was released, 24 staged
  shapes were created, and the refusal landed on the 25th.
- A one-line refusal record in `ExcelShapeWriter.Create` (added for this diagnosis and
  kept) named it exactly:
  `gantt.shape create-refused id=G-<token>:panel-cell:Description kind=Rectangle
  refusal=InvalidGeometry step=validate b=518.362,89,45,0`.

**Root cause.** `PanelRowProjection` projects every source row, Excel reports an
outline-hidden row's height as zero, and `PanelCellGrid` accepts it (ADR-0034).
`PanelBuilder` therefore emitted a `45x0` panel cell, and `ExcelShapeWriter.Validate`
refuses a non-positive extent before any host call. The live chart never hit it because
the live profile carries no data panel.

**Fix ([ADR-0067](../adr/0067-hidden-rows-contribute-no-panel-primitives.md)).**
`PanelBuilder` emits no `panel-cell:`, `panel-text:` or `panel-border-h:` primitive for a
zero-height row; the row keeps its positional slot and its zero height, so no later row
moves and the panel bounds are unchanged. Plot-area shapes are untouched — the owner's
ruling is that a collapsed row keeps its chart content and loses only its data-panel row.
`Visible=false` still reproduces its panel row (guide §4).

**Message defect fixed with it.** A composition refusal now names the typed
`ShapeWriteRefusal` (`TargetProtected`, `AlreadyExists`, `InvalidGeometry`, …), an
`ExportRefused` composition reuses the export-bounds sentence, and no copy refusal advises
a Refresh — which cannot delete a staging shape and cannot make a zero-area primitive
creatable. The old sentence sent the user in a circle.

**Evidence.** `PanelBuilderTests` 28/28 (new hidden-row fact), `ExportBoundsResolverTests`
19/19 (new no-non-positive-extent guard), `CopyEditableCommandTests` 39/39 (typed-refusal
mapping, no-Refresh wording, named staging shapes), `ExcelShapeWriterTests` 75/75 (the
refusal record). The live reproduction on the owner's workbook is the remaining gate: click
Copy editable, then paste into Excel and PowerPoint.

## Geometry parity — increment 1 (2026-11-19)

The copy pasted and ungrouped, and the owner compared it with the chart: the splitter fill
painted across the data panel, the title sat centred above every band, and the delineator
description label took the wrong corner. The audit against the refresh engine
([`../../plans/R6.8-export-live-geometry-parity-plan.md`](../../plans/R6.8-export-live-geometry-parity-plan.md))
found seven divergences; ADR-0068 records the five implemented here.

- **Splitter band spans the plot** (`SplitterBuilder`); `PanelLeftPt` stays the
  `DataPanelLeft` label field.
- **Drawn title is the live title** (`FrameBandsBuilder`): panel left edge and width on the
  year band's row. Bounds no longer grow by a title band and the export reserves none.
- **Anchor row carried** into the export grid, with the frame's bottom margin extended by it,
  so the overhang and closing line exist as on the sheet.
- **Panel border width** from the workbook's `GridLinePt` (`ExportPanelTheme`).

- **Lane anchoring** (the one-row drift): anchoring is now a composition rule
  (`EditableExportRequest` anchors, `ExportBoundsResolver` refuses an unanchored request). The
  `UnresolvableLaneAnchor` that blocked the first attempt was a fixture artefact: the fixture
  numbers rows by worksheet row while `GanttEvent.RowNumber` is the reader's one-based body
  index, which the resolver indexes the grid with. The fixture re-bases them, and the
  collapsed-rows fact collapses the two `CriticalInterval` children — a collapsed row is a
  child drawing on its parent's lane, never a hidden lane owner.

**Not yet in force:** the delineator label corner, re-measured on the next live copy because
the anchor-row overhang is expected to restore `Auto`'s room below the line.

**Evidence.** Core scene suites **73/73** (`SplitterBuilderTests`, `FrameBandsBuilderTests`,
`ExportBoundsResolverTests`); Office contract **89/89** (`ExcelSceneBuildRequestFactoryTests`,
`StagingLifecycleTests`, plus the two new facts). Office host gate: **Required and owed** —
a fresh copy compared against the chart.
