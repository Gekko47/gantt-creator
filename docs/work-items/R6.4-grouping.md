deterministic child order** (the scene's order), and the group
remains fully editable after ungroup in Excel — pinned by a
group-membership/child-order contract test and a live

## Design decisions (behavioural)

- **D1 — Group exactly the temporary set.** Membership = every staged
  shape, nothing else (an unowned shape accidentally grouped fails
  the test — **positive test** with a sentinel fixture).
- **D2 — Child order = scene order.** The group's child order is
  asserted against the staged creation/scene order; Office re-order
  quirks (if any) are probed at Tier-A upgrade and pinned — never
  silently accepted.
- **D3 — Editability preserved.** After ungroup (inspection), every
  expected child remains an editable shape with its geometry/style
  intact — the "reliably editable shape group" premise, proven not
  assumed.

## Stop conditions (item-specific)

- The host cannot preserve child order through grouping → stop;
  record evidence; the export's deterministic-order guarantee needs
  an ADR decision, not a shrug.
- Grouping corrupts child editability on a supported build → stop
  (the architecture's premise fails → scope-change path).

## Risk and rollback

directly) — probe at Tier-A upgrade; assert through whatever
deterministic surface exists (indices/names). Rollback: `git revert`.
