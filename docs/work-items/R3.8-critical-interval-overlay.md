## Design decisions

- **D1 — Overlay geometry (§16).** Per validated child with a resolved
  parent bar: X span = intersection of [own inclusive span] with
  [parent's **visible** (post-plot-clip) span], then clipped to
  `PlotBounds`; vertically the overlay sits on the parent bar's band
  per §16's top-edge position at `CriticalLinePt` thickness; stroke
  `CriticalStroke`; z-layer 50; ID `<rowId>:critical`.
- **D2 — Out-of-parent policy (checklist C).** Portions outside the
  parent's visible span clip with exactly one warning per child (never
  silently dropped, never drawn outside the parent). A child entirely
  outside emits no overlay + one warning (**positive test**).
- **D3 — Multiple children.** Disjoint/adjacent/overlapping children
  each produce their own overlay (no merging); overlaps order
  deterministically (lane/stack/sort/ID at layer 50) and may emit the
  §9-style ambiguity warning; adjacent intervals share an edge X
  exactly — no gap, no double line.
- **D4 — Orphans are impossible** (R2.5 blocks `ParentUnknown`);
  defence-in-depth still refuses an orphan with a typed outcome
  (**positive test**) rather than throwing.
