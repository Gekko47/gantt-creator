## Outcome

Every entity carries a Core-resolved `EntityProjection` naming its render-lane
owner and projection mode, `SourceRowVisible` is separate from `RenderVisible`, a
collapsed child still renders, and Excel never decides which lane an entity
belongs to.

## Scope

- New `src/GanttCreator.Core/Scene/EntityProjection.cs` — `EntityProjection`,
  `ProjectionMode`, `RenderLaneOwnerId`, and the capability matrix
- New `src/GanttCreator.Core/Scene/ProjectionResolver.cs` — Core resolution and
  its typed refusal
- `src/GanttCreator.Core/GanttEvent.cs` — `RenderVisible`, `SourceRowVisible`
- `src/GanttCreator.Core/Scene/LaneLayoutBuilder.cs` — consume resolved lane

## Exclusions

- **No row-height or outline work** — R4.7D.

## Design decisions

- **D1 — Projection is resolved in Core, before scene construction.** Excel must
  not infer it. This is the rule that stops the worksheet becoming a second
  source of truth for placement.
- **D2 — The modes are `OwnLane`, `OverlayParent`, `StackOnParent`,
  `MilestoneOnParent`, `PlotGlobal`, and a structural mode** for
  `Splitter`/`Spacer` (ADR-0021 made them lane participants).
- **D3 — Mapping:** top-level activity and top-level milestone → `OwnLane`;
  Critical Interval child → `OverlayParent`; child activity → `StackOnParent`;
  child milestone → `MilestoneOnParent`; `Delineator` → `PlotGlobal`;
  `Splitter`/`Spacer` → structural.
- **D4 — Render-lane ownership is derived**, not read: a top-level owner resolves
  to its own `Id`; a projected child resolves to its parent's lane owner.
  Contradictory `ParentId`/`LaneId` state is refused rather than resolved silently
  (REV5 §6).
- **D5 — `SourceRowVisible` and `RenderVisible` are separate members.** A
  collapsed child is `SourceRowVisible = false`, `RenderVisible = true`. **An
  Excel `EntireRow.Hidden` caused by hierarchy collapse is never mapped to
  `GanttEvent.Visible = false`** — that conflation is the single most likely way
  to make collapse silently drop a child from the chart.
- **D6 — Expand/collapse invariant:** it may change source-row visibility and
  later lane Y positions, and must not change whether a child renders, its dates,
  style, identity, or parent relationship. A test asserts this across both states.
- **D7 — A projected child creates no lane and no lane height.** Several children
  may overlap one parent lane. `StackIndex` orders them; it never grows a lane.
- **D8 — The capability matrix lives in Core and names only Types that exist in
  `EntityTypeCatalog`.** A second type list is forbidden. Every matrix member has
  a test.

## Acceptance tests

- **Automated** — `dotnet test tests/GanttCreator.Core.Tests`:
  - each `ProjectionMode` resolves for its documented Types
  - every `EntityTypeCatalog` member is either lane-owning or projected, and the
    matrix is exhaustive — a missing member fails
  - a child resolves to its parent's lane owner; a top-level row resolves to its own
  - a **collapsed** child builds a scene containing its primitive
  - `SourceRowVisible = false` with `RenderVisible = true` renders; a genuinely
    render-disabled entity does not
  - a contradictory `ParentId`/`LaneId` state is refused (positive test)
  - two children on one parent overlap without either growing the lane
  - **golden regeneration required** — this changes scene geometry. Human review
    and a stated reason; the regeneration ships in this commit per R3.17 D3.
- **Office host**: none for this row; R4.7D carries the live lane-alignment gate.

## Step 0 — verify prerequisites

- **R4.7A must have landed.** `EntityProjection.SourceEntityId` is a `GanttRowId`,
  and parent resolution depends on the hierarchy resolver R4.7A introduces.
- `LaneLayoutBuilder` currently derives lane order from the visible row position
  and `EffectiveStackIndex`. Re-read it before changing what it consumes.
- `ScenePrimitive.CreateId(SceneOwnerId, role)` and `GanttScene.TryCreate`'s
  duplicate-ID refusal are the identity guarantees a projected child relies on.

## Stop conditions

- Projection cannot be resolved without reading a hidden column as authoritative
  → stop.
- A second type list appears anywhere → stop; `EntityTypeCatalog` is the only one.

## Risk and rollback

Main risk: the golden changes in a way that looks like a regression. Mitigated by
reviewing the diff field-by-field and stating the reason in the commit, per the
golden policy. Rollback: `git revert`.
