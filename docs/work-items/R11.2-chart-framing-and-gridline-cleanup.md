# R11.2 — Chart framing cleanup: splitter fill only, plot grid retirement, seam and header-edge ownership

- **Status:** In progress (implemented 2026-11-27; Office host gate pending)
- **Phase:** 11 (post-R10 visual-contract maintenance)
- **Owner rulings:** 2026-11-27 (all four workstreams plus the three follow-up answers)
- **ADRs:** [`0074-splitter-band-is-a-fill-only`](../adr/0074-splitter-band-is-a-fill-only.md), [`0075-retire-the-plot-grid`](../adr/0075-retire-the-plot-grid.md), [`0076-plot-edges-and-header-cell-edges`](../adr/0076-plot-edges-and-header-cell-edges.md)
- **Skill index:** [`gantt-creator-contract`](../../.roo/skills/gantt-creator-contract/SKILL.md)

## Outcome

Four visible faults are corrected together because they all come from the same place — the scene
drawing lines nobody asked for:

1. A `Splitter` row is a **filled band only**. Its two `MajorBoundaryPt` rules are gone.
2. The plot contains **no interior vertical lines**. The alternating bands are the period
   separation, and `ShowMinorGrid`, `ShowMajorGrid` and `MinorGridColour` are retired.
3. The plot's own left and right edges are **unconditional**, so the plot's border is a complete
   rectangle in every composition.
4. The table/plot seam carries **one** vertical rule, and each header edge is drawn **once**.

## Acceptance criteria

- `SplitterBuilder` emits exactly one `SceneRect` at `ZLayer.Section` plus zero, one or two
  labels, and `SplitterRequest` no longer carries `BorderStyle`/`BorderWidthPt`.
- `AddGrid` emits two edge lines, `chart:plot:top` and `chart:plot:bottom`, and nothing else;
  `chart:grid:{date}` no longer exists and `AddGridLine`/`PeriodKey` are deleted.
- `FrameBandsRequest` and `SceneBuilder` no longer carry `GridLinePt`, `ShowMinorGrid` or
  `ShowMajorGrid`; `FrameBandsTheme` no longer carries `MinorGrid`.
- `GanttCatalogues` no longer defines `ShowMinorGrid`, `ShowMajorGrid`, `MinorGridColour` or
  `GridLinePt`; `GanttChartChromeRole.MinorGrid` is removed; the theme dialog offers one border
  width; `ExportPanelTheme` reads `MajorBoundaryPt`.
- `GanttSchemaVersion.CurrentSchemaVersion` is 30 and a version-29 workbook reports
  confirmation-required `SchemaVersionOlder` (`ExtraKey` from `AdditiveSchemaUpgrade`), with
  Repair as the remedy.
- `PanelBuilder` emits N vertical borders for N columns; the outer right edge belongs to the
  plot's edge line.
- Each year/period cell draws its left edge, only the last cell draws a right edge, the year row
  owns the top and the year/period seam, and the period row owns no horizontal edge.
- The scene golden is regenerated with this delta, recorded in the commit: **16 primitives
  removed, 0 added, 0 changed** — 3 interior grid lines, 12 period-cell border lines (the
  period row's top/bottom and the non-last right edges) and the splitter's 2 rules.
